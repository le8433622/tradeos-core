> This is a sample procurement-intelligence report, not an EOI submission, bid, quotation, legal opinion, supplier qualification, or statement of UN endorsement. No supplier's confidential information is used.

## 1. Executive decision

UNMISS has published an EOI for the supply and delivery of Audio Visual Devices in South Sudan. The notice requires vendors to be registered in UNGM under UNSPSC **52161600**, to provide their UNGM identification number and company contact details, and to submit the EOI through UNGM.

The official PDF does not specify a bill of quantities, detailed equipment list, model, technical standard, delivery address, delivery term, warranty requirement or pricing basis in the accessible text. Therefore, a supplier's generic AV/broadcast business profile is not enough to establish product fit or delivery feasibility.

**Recommendation:** A genuinely interested supplier may decide to submit an EOI only after confirming its own UNGM registration and legal entity. VDI cannot recommend a positive product fit at this stage. Classify as **HOLD / insufficient public specification** until UNMISS releases the solicitation or clarifies requirements.

## 6. Supplier EOI readiness checklist

Before deciding whether to submit, supplier should confirm:

- [ ] Exact legal entity that will submit and contract.
- [ ] Current legal registration and authorized signatory.
- [ ] Active UNGM account and UNGM ID for that same entity.
- [ ] UNSPSC 52161600 recorded in UNGM account.
- [ ] Company name, address, telephone and email match the legal / UNGM record.
- [ ] Authorized representative has access to the EOI form.
- [ ] Supplier has retained proof of successful EOI submission.
- [ ] Supplier understands that EOI is not a bid or award and does not guarantee invitation.

The supplier must submit its own EOI under its own eligible legal entity and account unless a separately authorized and lawful agency arrangement exists. VDI does not submit on behalf of the supplier.

## 7. What cannot be concluded

The public notice does **not** support conclusions about:
- Exact AV equipment types, models, quantities or specifications.
- Whether microphones, mixers, cameras, projectors, displays, conferencing equipment, speakers or accessories are required in this particular procurement.
- The exact delivery address, Incoterms edition, customs/import arrangements or unloading responsibility.
- Budget, tender value, unit price, expected gross margin or supplier capacity required.
- Which supplier will be invited, awarded or paid.

Do not create a price or landed-cost model from assumptions. Do not use a generic UNSPSC category as a substitute for technical matching.

**Sample decision for this notice: HOLD.** The public notice is sufficiently clear to check the EOI route and UNGM data requirements, but insufficient to verify product-level fit, logistics feasibility or bid pricing. A supplier may still independently submit an EOI if it is eligible and wishes to be considered.
