## 1. Commercial objective

Convert public procurement notices into paid supplier-fit and sourcing work for VDI.

- **Payer to validate:** Vietnamese manufacturers/exporters or qualified regional distributors that need a documented fit check and bid-readiness pack.
- **Buyer loss reduced:** time lost on irrelevant notices, rejected submissions, unverified product fit, and underestimated landed cost.
- **Paid unit:** one notice-fit assessment; one EOI-readiness pack; one evidence-backed supplier comparison.
- **AI/operator work:** read notice, map products and UNSPSC, identify potential suppliers, collect proof, compare delivery feasibility and prepare a decision report.
- **Evidence:** official notice/PDF, product page, company record, current certificates, UNGM registration evidence, written supplier confirmation, logistics quote and approval record.
- **Human approval boundary:** supplier outreach, sharing company information, EOI submission, bid/price commitment, payment, contract and production changes require explicit human approval.
- **Do not build yet:** defense marketplace, military hardware catalogue, inventory financing, or automated bid submission.

## 4. Supplier evidence checklist

A supplier may move from `PROSPECT` to `VERIFIED_FOR_NOTICE` only after the assigned operator has recorded:

1. Legal name, registration number, registered address, beneficial ownership and authorized signatory.
2. UNGM ID and registration level, verified against the supplier's own UNGM account evidence.
3. Manufacturer/distributor status and manufacturer-of-record for each quoted SKU.
4. Country of origin and supporting origin documents; do not infer origin from the supplier's address.
5. Exact SKU, product datasheet, specification match and deviations.
6. Current certificates, test reports, expiry dates, issuing body and scope.
7. SDS / dangerous-goods declarations where relevant; transport classification for chemical products.
8. Production capacity, available stock, MOQ, lead time, packing method and warranty.
9. Export references, customs documentation capability and Incoterms responsibility.
10. Written logistics quote to DAP UNMISS Logistics Base, Juba, with assumptions, exclusions, validity and transit estimate.

Never represent a self-declaration or web page as an independent verification.

## 7. Cost model

For a later priced solicitation, calculate:

`SellerDAPCost = item cost + export packing + export clearance + origin handling + main freight + transit handling + destination handling contractually borne by seller + inland haulage + delivery coordination to the named point + contingency`

For the Cleaning notice use Incoterms 2010 as stated in its PDF; for Fire, the broad Various Category notice and the separate Packaging Materials notice use Incoterms 2020. Do not silently normalize different Incoterms editions. Under DAP, keep the buyer/consignee's import clearance, duties/taxes and unloading costs in a separate cost column unless the contract specifically reallocates them.

No quantity, bill of quantities or final price is assumed at EOI stage. Do not invent a tender value or gross margin. The quote must state the basis and exclusions.
