# VDI Tender Fit Check — Commercial Offer Draft (Internal Only)

**Status:** Internal pricing / product hypothesis; not an offer, contract, invoice or authority to collect payment.  
**Version date:** 18 November 2026  
**Initial market:** Vietnam manufacturers, exporters and qualified distributors considering UN / NGO / international procurement opportunities.  
**Project status:** VDI is currently an independent B2B sourcing / procurement-intelligence project and has not been incorporated as a standalone legal entity.

## 1. Product promise

VDI sells evidence-backed decision support before a supplier spends time and money pursuing an international procurement notice.

The customer receives a documented answer to four questions:

1. Does the public notice appear relevant to the supplier's actual products/services and eligible legal entity?
2. Which requirements are explicitly evidenced, which are missing, and which require clarification?
3. What must the supplier prepare before deciding whether to submit an EOI or participate in a later solicitation?
4. What commercial and delivery risks should be resolved before any price or commitment is made?

VDI does not sell access to UN buyers, preferential treatment, a guarantee of EOI acceptance, an invitation, contract award, payment, or any claim of UN endorsement.

## 2. Three paid units to test

All amounts are pilot price hypotheses in VND. They are not approved tariffs and must be validated in real buyer conversations.

| Product | Pilot price hypothesis | Standard deliverable | Acceptance test |
|---|---:|---|---|

Optional DAP / landed-cost feasibility is a separately scoped engagement, not part of the above fixed prices by default. It requires exact SKU, quantity, packaging dimensions, gross weight, dangerous-goods status, origin, consignee/importer-of-record and named delivery point. Third-party quotes are passed through only with the customer's prior written approval.

## 3. Commercial boundaries

- VDI acts as an independent research / sourcing coordination service, not as UNMISS, UNGM, a procurement officer, buyer, tender agent or approved UN supplier.
- Supplier remains responsible for its legal eligibility, product conformity, origin claims, declarations, prices, bid bonds, warranties, delivery and submission.
- VDI does not create or access a supplier's UNGM account, submit an EOI in the supplier's name, fabricate qualifications, or imply that a public notice is an award.
- No guarantee of invitation, solicitation, award, order, payment, or revenue.
- No undisclosed commission, kickback, referral payment or conflict. Any success fee or third-party compensation would require explicit written disclosure, lawful contracting, and prior customer approval; do not offer by default.
- Exclude weapons, ammunition, explosives, combat equipment, surveillance, tactical targeting and other combat-enabling items. Initial scope is civilian-grade, non-lethal supplies and services only.
- Do not receive, hold, forward, or spend customer or supplier funds on their behalf during the pilot.

## 4. Pilot delivery workflow

1. **Intake:** Customer supplies legal entity name, website, product/service catalogue, target notice and consent to review information.
2. **Source lock:** VDI saves the official notice, linked documents, retrieval date, deadline and timezone; conflicting sources are explicitly flagged.
3. **Fit map:** Map exact notice line items/UNSPSC to supplier catalogue. No category-level assumption is enough for a positive SKU match.
4. **Evidence check:** Separate official/third-party evidence from supplier self-declaration. Record gaps and contradictory claims.
5. **Risk review:** Check UNGM/legal entity, certifications and scope, export readiness, Incoterms, DAP importer-of-record, delivery-site access and logistics.
6. **Recommendation:** GO / HOLD / NO-GO with reasons and unresolved conditions.
7. **Handover:** Deliver PDF or signed report plus evidence register, with one review meeting.
8. **Customer decision:** Supplier decides whether to register, submit an EOI or participate in a later solicitation. VDI does not submit on its behalf.

## 5. Service levels and exclusions

- Work starts only after a valid written engagement, authorized contracting party and agreed scope are in place.
- Target turnaround should be agreed per engagement after the scope and source documents are known; do not promise a universal turnaround before testing workload.
- No legal opinion, tax opinion, engineering certification, laboratory testing, customs ruling, freight quotation, security clearance or government authorization is included unless a separately qualified provider is engaged and identified.
- Public-source screening is not a full background check or beneficial-ownership investigation.
- Supplier-declared facts remain labelled as such until independent documentary validation.
- Third-party translations, testing, legal review, courier, registry extracts, site inspection and travel are excluded unless approved in writing in advance.

## 6. Payment and contracting controls — not yet active

Before any external offer, contract, invoice or payment request:
1. Identify the eligible legal entity that will contract with the customer and be responsible for service delivery.
2. Confirm authority of the signatory, applicable tax/invoicing obligations, bank account ownership and permitted business activity with qualified local advisers.
3. Approve the final scope, deliverable, price, payment milestones, cancellation/refund policy, confidentiality, data handling and liability cap in writing.
4. Keep client money and any supplier money separate; do not accept a success fee without disclosure and signed terms.
5. Record invoice number, payment evidence and recognized revenue separately from GMV, indicative tender value and unawarded pipeline.

**Current gate:** Not cleared. VDI is not an incorporated standalone legal entity. No offer or invoice may be issued by VDI under this draft until the contracting-entity gate is resolved.

## 7. Pilot success metrics

Measure outcomes rather than email volume:

- Paid conversion: number of paid engagements / qualified customer proposals.
- Evidence completeness: required fields supported by current documents / total required fields.
- Fit precision: proportion of positive matches upheld after supplier technical review.
- Customer decision usefulness: percentage of reports resulting in a documented GO / HOLD / NO-GO.
- Delivery margin: collected fee less attributable labour, data, translation and approved third-party costs.
- Trust metric: corrections, unsupported claims, missed deadlines, complaints and undisclosed conflicts (target: zero).

Do not count email sends, supplier prospects, tender headline values, supplier GMV or unawarded pipeline as VDI revenue.

## 8. Initial proof asset

Use the UNMISS Juba campaign as a sample report only after removing private email addresses, direct contact details, supplier correspondence IDs and any non-public data. Public notices may be linked; each supplier statement must be clearly marked self-reported until verified.
